How it works

A whole week of small nudges — so the date actually happens.

When both partners swipe right on the same date, DuoSpark takes over.

Sunday
9:00 AM · Date Deck arrives
Three personalized ideas, both swipe privately.
Match found when you both pick the same one. Auto-scheduled.

9:00 AM · Shopping list
Personalized list — for your city, your diet.
Amazon links + cost estimates. Two lists for LDR couples.
